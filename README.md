1. Game Title: “0-Zone”
   
2. Game Genre: Action, Combat, Horror
   
3. Game Description:
A paranormal expert is tasked with exploring an abandoned orphanage that suffered a tragic fire decades ago.
Upon investigation, spectral enemies appear, trapping you inside.
Escape the orphanage while fighting ghost enemies and dodging tricky obstacles.

4. Game Objective
The player must: 
- Survive attacks from spectral enemies.
- Navigate through platforming obstacles and environmental hazards.
- Search the orphanage for weapons, ammunition, and health gear.
- Defeat or avoid ghost enemies.
- Find the key needed to unlock the final door.
- Reach the safe zone/exit without losing all health.

5. Core Gameplay
The player progresses through different areas of the abandoned orphanage by moving, jumping, and navigating platforms.
During exploration, the player encounters various spectral enemies.
Weapons and ammunition can be found throughout designated areas of the map.
Health gear can also be collected to restore the player's health.
The player must carefully manage their health, ammunition, and movement while dealing with enemies and environmental obstacles.

6. Main Game Elements
Player: Paranormal Expert
The player character is an experienced paranormal investigator equipped to deal with supernatural threats.
Enemies/Obstacles: 
Crawling Fully Burnt Ghosts (swarm in numbers)
Collectibles/Items: Ammunition, and Health Gear, and the Key for the last door
Goal: Escape the abandoned location alive without running out of health.
